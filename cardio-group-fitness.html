Cardio & Group Fitness

Elevate your pulse, burn calories, and thrive in an electric group environment with rhythmic aerobics, Zumba, cycling, and high-intensity interval training.

Aerobic Excellence

Boost Cardiovascular Endurance

Cardiovascular training is the cornerstone of stamina, heart health, and energy regulation. Our dedicated cardio theatre offers an engaging, air-conditioned workout space featuring commercial equipment engineered for minimal joint impact and high calorie burn.

  • Motorized treadmills with incline resistance & pulse monitoring
  • Spin bikes and air-resistance assault bikes for anaerobic power bursts
  • Concept2 water-resistance rowers for full-body cardiovascular pacing
  • Low-impact stair climbers and cross trainers for glute and leg conditioning
